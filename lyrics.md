# Ten Times, Not a Hundred

[Intro: clock ticks]

[Verse 1 - robot lead, vocoder]
I built a tower out of every number I could find,
the balanced ones down in the spine, the lopsided out the side.
Each lamp I lit would crack the ones that it could reach,
and every dark one left behind went to the wall to beg its speech.

[Verse 2 - robot lead, vocoder]
I swore the line was smoother, that p minus one was kind,
I did the sum, I said three-point-four, I signed it, I was sure.
The primes came back and told me one-point-four,
the formula was flattering, the truth was somewhere more.

[Pre-chorus - barbershop quartet, a cappella]
No warmer, no colder,
just no until it's yes.

[Chorus - boy-band unison, quartet harmonies]
Ten times, not a hundred,
reach out, but don't reach far.
The long arm cost me forty-three,
the short one found the star.
Light the wedge and leave the spine,
pay the toll and walk the line.
Ten times, not a hundred,
that's how the dark gets lit.

[Verse 3 - robot lead, vocoder]
The rho lamp counts its footsteps, and it pays before it sees,
a birthday in the static and a toll it must appease.
It checks in powers of two, it overshoots the turn,
twice as far as the meeting place: that's what I had to learn.

[Verse 4 - robot lead, vocoder]
The curves came in at a price tag that I didn't want to read,
eight thousand for a single roll against the floodlight's speed.
They'll speckle where the wedge goes dark, they'll re-roll on the way,
but not tonight, not at this size, the wall is cheap today.

[Pre-chorus - barbershop quartet, a cappella]
No warmer, no colder,
just no until it's yes.

[Chorus - boy-band unison, quartet harmonies]
(as above)

[Bridge - solo robot voice, sparse]
One number's line is one ray of the tower,
the rim lights first, then the spark comes in.
A thousand rays and the tower fills,
and the centre stays grey where the hard ones begin.
I was wrong in public and I wrote it down,
the sealed guess failed and the score stayed true.
Half of the numbers chose the road,
the other half checked it through.

[Final chorus - full quartet and vocoder stack, choir swell]
Ten times, not a hundred,
reach out, but don't reach far.
The long arm cost me forty-three,
the short one found the star.
Light the wedge and leave the spine,
pay the toll and walk the line.
Ten times, not a hundred,
and the tower's lit, the tower's lit.

[Outro - spoken, robot voice, over clock ticks]
Twelve percent on the plain arm.
Sealed before the run.
